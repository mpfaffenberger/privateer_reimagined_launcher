FAQ

Why do I need the original game?

Privateer Reimagined builds on Origin's 1993 game, including its music and voices. Owning the original is how we respect that. Pick it up on GOG if you don't have it.

How big are updates?

Only changed files are downloaded, usually a fraction of the full install.

Is this an official EA or Origin product?

No. It's a non-commercial fan project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts. Wing Commander and Privateer are trademarks of their owners.
