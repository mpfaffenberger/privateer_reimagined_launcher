Every ship in the Gemini Sector is one you can fly, from a merchant's Tarsus to a Kilrathi capital ship, each rebuilt as a new 3D model. Every shot here was staged in the game.

The story you remember, retold

The original campaign is back, with new cinematics and a cast you can actually see. Haul cargo, take mercenary contracts, hunt bounties, or follow the trail of a strange alien artifact. The bars are busy again, and someone always has a job for you.

Install in four steps

  1. Download the launcher

    It's a 3 to 4 MB download. It doesn't need administrator rights.

  2. Open it

    Windows may show "Windows protected your PC", because the launcher isn't code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

    Mac: open the disk image and drag Privateer Reimagined into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it can't verify the app, because it isn't notarized by Apple yet. Click Done, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway, then confirm. You only do this once; updates install themselves.

    To check your download, its SHA-256 is for Windows: 43bb31bff15d780dc3ba89886ac303a5b6dded0cef8fd31af30d9e3631644f31 for Mac: 5ee44d12f2b793963b890a3eab3dc7db599d071dda74d064df19238238ed1407

  3. Show it your Privateer

    The launcher finds GOG and Steam copies of Wing Commander: Privateer on its own. If it can't, point it at the folder.

  4. Install and play

    The first install downloads about 3.4 GB. After that, updates fetch only the files that changed, check every byte, and never touch your saves.

You'll need

  • Wing Commander: Privateer on GOG or Steam
  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, or a Mac with Apple silicon
  • About 4 GB of free space

Questions

Why do I need the original game?

Privateer Reimagined builds on Origin's 1993 game, including its music and voices. Owning the original is how we respect that. It's on GOG and Steam if you don't have it.

How big are updates?

Usually a few megabytes. Each release is a signed list of files, and the launcher only downloads the ones that changed. It can also repair a damaged install.

Is the launcher safe to run?

Every file the launcher installs is checked against a list signed by the project, so it won't install anything that was tampered with on the way. The launcher itself isn't code-signed by Microsoft or notarized by Apple yet, which is why Windows and macOS warn you the first time. Compare its SHA-256 above if you'd like to check it.

Is this an official EA or Origin product?

No. It's a non-commercial fan project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Electronic Arts.
