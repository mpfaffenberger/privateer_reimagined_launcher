From the sector

  • 17 Oct

    Game 0.1.7. The Ship Dealer now sells the Talon (75,000 credits) and the Demon (175,000). Ships flying close together no longer jiggle: they only bump when their hulls truly meet, and a bump knocks them once instead of shaking them. And the big man on the foreground stool has left the bar, in every base that shares it.

  • 17 Oct

    Game 0.1.6. Snappier turning: your ship reaches the turn you ask for in about a third of a second instead of two-thirds, and settles just as quickly. In New Detroit's bar, the front booth now waits empty until Sandoval or Tayla has work for you, and the refinery bar's foreground stool is free.

  • 16 Oct

    Join us on Discord. Get help, report bugs, pitch ideas and share screenshots with other pilots: discord.gg/9Vhm4tdymY

  • 16 Oct

    Game 0.1.5. Fly with a joystick or HOTAS: under Options, Controls binds pitch, yaw, roll, throttle, fire and afterburner, with calibration, deadzones, inversion and response curves for each axis, and remembers your setup. The Broadsword has a new silver-gunmetal cockpit with its three guns out front. And in New Detroit's bar, Sandoval and Tayla wait in the front booth when they have work for you; Oxford's bar finally has a bartender.

  • 10 Oct

    Game 0.1.4. Pick your difficulty under Options, on the title screen or in the Esc menu. Each step toward Easy weakens enemy guns and shields by 10% and makes yours 10% stronger, up to 50% at Easy. Hard is the reverse. Medium is the default, and your choice is saved with your game.

  • 6 Oct

    Game 0.1.3. Press Esc for a pause menu: load a save, start over, quit, or look up every control. On a base, Esc now opens the menu; use Launch to take off. And the pirate ambush near Troy is gone.

  • 5 Oct

    Now on Mac. Game 0.1.2 and launcher 0.3.2 run on Macs with Apple silicon, and keep themselves up to date.

  • 4 Oct

    Launcher 0.3.0 updates itself from now on, and lets you play while offline.

  • 3 Oct

    Launcher 0.2.0. News from the project now shows up right here.

  • 3 Oct

    Game 0.1.1 is out. The launcher keeps your copy up to date by itself.
