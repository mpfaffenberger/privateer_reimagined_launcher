  • 17 Oct

    Game 0.1.9. The Talon has a brand-new cockpit, with a titanium bubble canopy and a round gyro screen under the radar: a live hologram of your ship that banks, pitches and turns with you. It fires the thrusters for every manoeuvre, flares on afterburner, shows each gun firing in its own colour, flashes the side that takes a hit, and warns you of incoming missiles. Every cockpit now has its own HUD colour, shields and armour included. The Demon's cockpit skulls are right side up and its guns fire from the right barrels. The landing pad shows the ship you just bought, and pilots talk with their original voice lines again.

  • 17 Oct

    Game 0.1.8. Bounty targets now hide out at a nav point in the system they were last seen in, so you'll have to hunt for them. The ship dealer's salesman walks every showroom, not just Oxford's.
